About Us

Family-Run, Built on Hard Work

DIG and MOVE Earthworks is a family business that takes pride in doing the job right, every time. Based in Sunbury, servicing Melbourne and Gippsland.

Our Story

Earthworks Done Right, Every Time

DIG and MOVE Earthworks is a family-run business based in Sunbury, VIC, servicing across Melbourne, surrounding suburbs, and the Gippsland region. We're not locked into one narrow lane - we cover all types of earthworks, from site cuts and dam cleans through to footings, pier holes, and material load outs.

We position ourselves on professional service and customer satisfaction. Whether it's a residential build, a rural property, or a commercial site, we bring the same level of care and precision to every job. We show up on time, get the work done properly, and leave the site clean.

Being a family business means we treat every client's project like it's our own. We're always available, flexible with scheduling, and easy to get in touch with - whether that's by phone, email, or Facebook Messenger.
