Earthmoving & Haulage

Moving, spreading, and removing material on site. Whether it's topsoil for landscaping or rock and concrete that needs to go, we handle the heavy lifting across Sunbury, Melbourne, and Gippsland.

Moving Material, Getting Results

The Heavy Lifting, Sorted

Every earthworks project involves moving material. Whether it's spreading topsoil across a new block, loading out excess dirt from a site cut, or removing rock and concrete from a demolition, the material needs to go somewhere and it needs to be handled efficiently.

We handle earthmoving and haulage across Sunbury, Melbourne's suburbs, and the Gippsland region. We work on residential, commercial, and rural sites, bringing the right machinery to move material quickly and keep the job on schedule.

Whether you need material brought in, spread across a site, or loaded out and removed, we coordinate the full process so you don't have to.

Soil Spreading

Even distribution of material across a site takes the right equipment and an experienced operator. We spread topsoil, fill, and other materials to the level and depth you need, whether it's a backyard, a building pad, or a large lot.

We work with builders, landscapers, and homeowners to get the ground prepared for the next stage. Whether you're filling a site before a build, spreading topsoil for turf, or levelling out a block, we spread it evenly and to spec.

Topsoil, fill, and aggregate spreading
Level and even distribution to spec
Backyards, building pads, and large lots
Site prep for turf, landscaping, and construction

Dirt, Rock & Concrete Load Outs

When material needs to leave the site, we handle the loading and removal. Whether it's excess dirt from a site cut, rock from excavation, or broken concrete from a demolition, we load it out efficiently and keep the site tidy throughout.

We coordinate the loading process to keep things moving. Material is loaded into trucks on site and removed promptly, so your project stays on schedule and the site remains safe and accessible for other trades.

Excess dirt, rock, and concrete removal
Efficient on-site loading into trucks
Site kept tidy and accessible throughout
Residential, commercial, and demolition sites

Lawn Prep

A great lawn starts with proper ground preparation. We strip the old surface, level the ground, spread topsoil to the right depth, and compact it so your turf or seed has the best possible base to establish on.

Whether you're laying turf on a new build, replacing an old lawn, or preparing a large area for seeding, we handle the heavy work so your landscaper or turf supplier can come in and finish the job.

Ground stripping, levelling, and grading
Topsoil spreading to the required depth
Compaction for a stable turf base
New builds, renovations, and large-scale turf projects
