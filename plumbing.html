Plumbing Services

Fully licenced plumbing for residential, commercial, and rural properties. From general maintenance and hot water systems to blocked drains and gas fitting, we handle all types of plumbing work.

Fully Licenced Plumber on the Team

Professional Plumbing You Can Trust

As well as earthworks, DIG and MOVE has a fully licenced plumber on the team. That means we can handle your plumbing needs alongside your earthmoving project, or as a standalone service.

Whether it's a new build that needs rough-in plumbing, a blocked drain that needs clearing, a hot water system that needs replacing, or general plumbing maintenance, we've got it covered. Having a licenced plumber in-house means less coordinating between trades and faster turnaround on your project.

We service Sunbury, Melbourne, and the Gippsland region for all plumbing work. Same professional service, same commitment to getting the job done right.

General Plumbing

From tap repairs and toilet replacements to full bathroom and kitchen plumbing, we cover all general plumbing work for homes and businesses. Whether it's a small fix or a full fit-out, we do it properly and to code.

We also handle rough-in plumbing for new builds and renovations, working alongside builders and other trades to keep the project moving. Having both earthworks and plumbing under one roof makes coordination simple.

Tap, toilet, and fixture repairs and replacements
Bathroom and kitchen plumbing
Rough-in plumbing for new builds and renovations
Leak detection and pipe repairs

Hot Water Systems

Whether your hot water system has broken down and needs replacing, or you're looking to upgrade to something more efficient, we can help. We install, repair, and service all types of hot water systems.

We'll assess your current setup, recommend the right system for your household or business, and get it installed properly. If it's an emergency, we'll get to you as quickly as possible.

Electric, gas, and solar hot water systems
Repairs, replacements, and new installations
System assessments and upgrade recommendations
Emergency hot water breakdowns

Blocked Drains & Drainage

Blocked drains are one of the most common plumbing issues, and they need to be sorted quickly before they cause bigger problems. We clear blocked drains, stormwater pipes, and sewer lines for homes and businesses.

We also handle drainage installation and repairs. Whether it's a new stormwater system for a build, agricultural drainage on a rural property, or fixing an existing drainage issue, we get it sorted properly.

Blocked drain clearing and cleaning
Stormwater and sewer line repairs
New drainage system installation
Agricultural and rural drainage solutions

Gas Fitting

Gas work needs to be done by a licenced professional, and that's exactly what we provide. We handle gas fitting for cooktops, heaters, hot water systems, barbecues, and gas line installations.

Safety is the priority with any gas work. We ensure every installation and repair meets all current regulations and is tested thoroughly before we sign off on it.

Gas cooktop, heater, and BBQ installations
Gas line installation and extensions
Gas leak detection and repairs
Compliance testing and certification
