Site Preparation

Everything you need to get a site ready for construction. From clearing and cutting to footings and concrete prep, we handle the groundwork so your build starts on solid foundations.

Getting It Right From the Ground Up

The Foundation of Every Good Build

Site preparation is where every project starts. Whether you're building a new home, pouring a slab, or putting up a deck, the groundwork needs to be right before anything else goes on top. That's what we do.

We handle site cuts and cleans, concrete prep, footing excavation, and pier hole drilling across Sunbury, Melbourne, and the Gippsland region. We work with builders, owner-builders, and homeowners to make sure the ground is level, compacted, and ready for the next trade to step in.

Every site is different. We assess the ground conditions, work to your engineer's specs, and make sure the finished result is accurate and ready to build on.

Site Cuts & Cleans

Before any construction can begin, the site needs to be cleared and cut to level. We remove vegetation, debris, and topsoil, then cut the ground to the required grade so you have a clean, level pad to build on.

Whether it's a residential block, a commercial lot, or a rural property, we handle the full scope of site clearing and cutting. We work to your engineer's plans and leave the site ready for the next stage of the build.

Vegetation removal and site clearing
Cut and fill to engineer's specifications
Residential, commercial, and rural sites
Clean pad ready for slab or construction

Concrete Prep

Getting the ground right before a concrete pour makes all the difference. We excavate to the required depth, level the base, and compact the subgrade so your slab, driveway, or path sits properly from day one.

Poor prep leads to cracking, settling, and problems down the track. We take the time to get the base right, working to your concreter's or engineer's specs so there are no surprises when it comes time to pour.

Excavation to required depth and dimensions
Base levelling and subgrade compaction
House slabs, driveways, paths, and shed pads
Work to concreter's or engineer's specs

Footings

Footings are the structural foundation of any build, so accuracy matters. We dig clean, precise trenches to the depths and widths specified by your engineer, ready for your concreters to step in and pour.

We work on new homes, extensions, retaining walls, and commercial buildings. Whether it's strip footings, pad footings, or something more specific, we dig to spec and leave the trenches clean with minimal disturbance to the surrounding ground.

Precise excavation to engineer's specs
Strip footings and pad footings
New homes, extensions, and retaining walls
Clean trenches with minimal ground disturbance

Pier Holes

Pier holes need to be the right depth, the right diameter, and in the right spot. We drill pier holes to your engineer's specifications for decks, pergolas, retaining walls, carports, and structural foundations.

We use auger attachments on our excavators to drill clean, accurate holes in a range of soil conditions. Whether it's a few holes for a backyard deck or a full set for a new build, we get them done quickly and to spec.

Accurate depth and diameter to spec
Decks, pergolas, carports, and retaining walls
Auger drilling in varied soil conditions
Structural foundations for new builds

Form Tracks

Form tracks provide solid, level access paths for concrete trucks, pumps, and other heavy vehicles that need to get around a construction site. Without proper form tracks, you risk bogging machinery, damaging the site, and slowing down the build.

We prepare and compact the ground to create stable access paths that hold up under heavy loads. Once the concrete pour is done and the tracks are no longer needed, we can remove and reinstate the area as part of the job.

Stable access for concrete trucks and pumps
Compacted and levelled to handle heavy loads
New builds, extensions, and commercial sites
Removal and reinstatement after the pour

Plumber Digs

Plumbers need clean, accurate trenches to lay pipes for water supply, sewer, stormwater, and gas lines. We dig to the required depth and width, keeping the trenches straight and the base level so the pipes can be laid properly.

We work alongside plumbers on new builds, renovations, and infrastructure projects. Whether it's a single trench for a house connection or a larger network of service trenches, we get them dug efficiently and to spec.

Trenches for water, sewer, stormwater, and gas
Accurate depth and width to plumber's specs
New builds, renovations, and service connections
Efficient excavation with minimal site disruption

Pool Digs

Installing a pool starts with getting the hole right. We excavate to the exact dimensions and depth required by your pool installer, working carefully around existing structures, fences, and landscaping to minimise disruption.

We handle the full excavation process, from the initial dig through to removing the spoil from site. Whether it's a small plunge pool or a full-sized swimming pool, we have the right machinery to get it done.

Excavation to pool installer's specifications
Careful work around existing structures
Spoil removal and site clean-up
All pool sizes from plunge pools to full-sized

Basements

Basement excavation is one of the more complex earthworks jobs. It requires careful planning, precise digging to engineer's specs, and proper management of the surrounding ground to protect neighbouring structures and services.

We excavate basements for residential and commercial builds, working to your engineer's drawings and shoring plans. We manage the spoil removal, maintain safe batter angles, and ensure the finished excavation is ready for the next stage of construction.

Excavation to engineer's drawings and specs
Residential and commercial basement digs
Spoil removal and site management
Safe batter angles and ground protection
